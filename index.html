Welcome to IISE

Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh

Education that opens
more than one door.

IISE is a learning community for aspiring professionals in management, information technology, media and commerce.

Established in 1991, the International Institute for Special Education is committed to developing capable, ethical and confident leaders. Its guiding philosophy remains: “Of the students, by the students, for the students.”
